Penpot recently introduced the plugins system (and started a challenge to submit plugins for some prizes) so I decided to build some plugins I found relatively useful.

You can find links to install them on Penpot as well as their source code on Github.

## Squircles

Generate squircle shapes for your designs.

[Source Code](https://github.com/iamnabholz/penpot-squircle-plugin)

## Confettier

Simple confetti generation.

[Source Code](https://github.com/iamnabholz/penpot-confettier-plugin)

## Gridify

Quickly generate dot and grid lines for your designs

[Source Code](https://github.com/iamnabholz/penpot-gridify-plugin)

## Tints and Shades

Generate darker or lighter variants of any color

[Source Code](https://github.com/iamnabholz/penpot-tints-and-shades-plugin)

## Skeleton Layout

Plugin to transform your designs to low-fidelity, skeleton versions.

Replace the text with basic shapes to create a full skeleton or simply change their color, keeping the content.

[Source Code](https://github.com/iamnabholz/penpot-skeleton-layout-plugin)

## Starter Profile

Generate avatars and text to fill profile information in your designs, avatars, names, countries, usernames, and dates.

[Source Code](https://github.com/iamnabholz/penpot-starter-profile-plugin)
